FUTURE ADDITIONS
----------------
When you spot more wildlife (butterflies, bees, snakes, frogs,
other birds), drop the photos in here and add a new <figure>
block in index.html. The grid will auto-arrange them.

Recommended photo size: at least 800px wide, landscape orientation
preferred. Square is fine. JPG or WebP both work.
