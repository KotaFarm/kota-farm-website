Crop journey
Bed preparation · Oct 28
Soil prepared with farm compost
Beds were turned and mixed with 2-month-old compost from our own pile. No external inputs. Mulched with dry grass from the farm to retain moisture before planting.
Sowing · Nov 12
Seedlings raised in the shade net nursery
Seeds from last season's best tomatoes — saved and dried. Germination took 8 days. Seedlings moved to field beds at 3-week stage.
Growth · Dec
Whitefly attack — handled without spray
Honest note: We had a whitefly problem in early December. Rather than spraying, we used yellow sticky traps and removed affected leaves by hand. Yield was ~20% lower than expected on the first flush, but no chemicals were used.
Harvest · Ongoing
Picking every 2–3 days
Hand-picked in the early morning. Sorted on-farm and dispatched same day. No cold storage — fresh means fresh.

Crop journey
Sowing · Dec 18
Direct sown in the herb bed
Seeds soaked for 12 hours before broadcast sowing. Lightly raked in. Our coriander seeds are from plants we let bolt and seed every year — the variety has adapted to our climate over several seasons.
Harvest · Ongoing
Cut-and-come-again, harvested weekly
Cut above the growth node so the plant regrows. Same planting gives 3–4 cuts. Picked early morning and bundled immediately. Reaches you within hours of harvest.
